## Question

Does Polaris cause a different and better attention, focus, or discard decision than would probably have happened without it?

## Why it might matter

Polaris is useful only if the system improves decisions rather than merely organizing information.

## Evidence

- [confirmed] Repository initialized on 2026-09-04.
- [hypothesis] Polaris may reduce useful uncertainty per unit of user attention.
- [hypothesis] Structured review may change attention decisions compared with unaided judgment.

## Uncertainty

No counterfactual evidence exists yet. The repository currently demonstrates the method's intended structure, not its causal effectiveness.

## Next experiment

Record review outcomes where Polaris materially changes a likely unaided attention decision, including the decision before and after the review when that comparison is available.
