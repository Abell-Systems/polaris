## Question

How can I reach enough financial freedom to reliably help my parents with their financial situation without destroying the capital and autonomy I am building for my own future?

## Why it might matter

My financial freedom is not only an individual objective. Part of its purpose is to give me the capacity to support my parents when they need it. Their current financial margin is very limited, so my own capital-building decisions need to account for this responsibility.

## Evidence

- [user assertion] My parents have approximately €16,000 remaining on the house.
- [user assertion] They have approximately €6,000 in the bank.
- [user assertion] They have approximately €5,000 in community-related debt.
- [user assertion] They generate only approximately €7,000 per year.
- [user assertion] They need my financial help.
- [user assertion] Part of my objective in achieving financial freedom is to be able to help my parents.
- [user assertion] I currently have approximately €38,000 in savings/patrimony.
- [user constraint] I want to preserve approximately €20,000 in liquid reserves rather than consuming all available capital.
- [inference] The parents' situation is therefore coupled to, rather than separate from, my own financial-freedom trajectory.

## Uncertainty

The key uncertainty is how to support my parents sustainably while continuing to build my own financial resilience. The immediate debt position, their low annual income, and the need to preserve my own liquidity make the timing and form of support consequential.

## Next experiment

Build a complete picture of the parents' liabilities, income, essential expenses, available assets, and repayment obligations. Then determine what level and form of support materially improves their resilience while preserving my own minimum liquidity and continued capital accumulation.
