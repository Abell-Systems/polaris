# Review

The purpose of review is to decide what deserves attention, not to produce a complete ranking.

## Inspect

Read the current `now` and `next` research lines, then inspect recent evidence and unresolved uncertainty. Look for overlap, convergence, opportunity cost, new external constraints, and changes in the consequences of being wrong.

## Optional DAFO

DAFO/SWOT may be used when it clarifies an argument:

- strengths — what makes the line unusually viable now
- weaknesses — internal constraints or missing capability
- opportunities — favorable openings exposed by current evidence
- threats — external conditions or failure modes

Do not assign scores or weights. Do not turn DAFO into a ranking mechanism.

## Output

Explain:

1. which lines appear to deserve attention and why;
2. which lines appear not to deserve attention now and why;
3. the key trade-off when attention between lines is mutually exclusive;
4. what unresolved evidence could change the recommendation.

The user chooses the attention decision.

## Effectiveness question

A successful review should be capable of changing the user's attention, focus, or discard decision. Record that outcome in telemetry when useful so Polaris can test its own value.
