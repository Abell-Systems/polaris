# Digest

A digest is a transversal view over recent changes in the uncertainty-and-attention map.

Focus on:

- investigations opened, reframed, consolidated, promoted, parked, or closed;
- meaningful evidence or uncertainty changes;
- decisions of attention and what drove them;
- notable changes in the operating method itself.

Do not summarize commit count, activity volume, or routine edits unless they materially affect the map.
